How it works: Border (USD) and Mexico (MXN) are two independent, always-saved calculations sharing the same Weight, Purchase Price, US Freight, Inspection, and Payment Days. Border uses your Selling Price (USD/lb) and Direct Costs = US Freight + Inspection. Mexico appears once you enter an Exchange Rate — it adds Mexican Freight (already MXN) + Import Cost (USD, converted with the Exchange Rate) and uses your Selling Price Mexico (MXN/kg), converting your USD purchase cost and shared US costs to MXN. Both results show at once — nothing is ever cleared, no toggle needed. Net Margin/Gross Margin formulas: Net = Profit / Sale · Gross = (Sale − Cost − Direct Costs) / Sale.
