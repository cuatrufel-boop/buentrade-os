Our Story

BuenTrade was born from years spent on the front lines of customer relationships and meat trading — watching deals get done, and noticing how often trust was the missing ingredient.

After more than five years working directly with clients across Mexico, sourcing beef, pork, and poultry from the U.S., Brazil, and Canada, our founder saw the same pattern again and again: businesses want a partner who follows through, communicates honestly, and treats every order like it matters — not just another transaction.

So BuenTrade was built around that idea. We started with animal protein for Mexico, and grew to include dry goods and consumer products for the Caribbean — always with the same principle guiding us: earn trust first, and the business follows.
